Meet Your Dentist

Emerson Vicuna, DDS

Doctor of Dental Surgery • Ohio State University

With over 20 years of experience, Dr. Vicuna combines expert clinical skills with a warm, personal approach. Originally from Lima, Peru, he earned his biology and chemistry degrees from UNM before completing his DDS at Ohio State. As the sole practitioner, every patient receives his personal attention.
