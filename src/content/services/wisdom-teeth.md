## What to Expect

Wisdom teeth removal is a routine procedure that prevents future dental problems. Dr. Vicuna will take X-rays to assess the position of your wisdom teeth and plan the extraction. The area is numbed completely, and the teeth are carefully removed. Most patients recover within a few days with the help of our detailed aftercare instructions.

## Benefits

- Prevents crowding and shifting of existing teeth
- Eliminates pain and discomfort from impacted wisdom teeth
- Reduces the risk of infection and cysts in the jaw
- Common, well-established procedure with predictable recovery
