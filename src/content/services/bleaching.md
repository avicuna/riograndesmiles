## What to Expect

Professional teeth whitening delivers dramatic results in a single visit. Dr. Vicuna will apply a professional-grade whitening gel to your teeth and monitor the process carefully. You can expect your teeth to brighten several shades in about an hour, with results that last for months.

## Benefits

- Noticeably whiter teeth in just one office visit
- Safe, professionally monitored treatment
- Boosts confidence and enhances your overall appearance
- Long-lasting results with proper maintenance
