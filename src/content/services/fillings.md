## What to Expect

Getting a filling is one of the most common dental procedures. Dr. Vicuna will gently numb the area, remove any decay, and place a tooth-colored composite filling that blends naturally with your smile. The entire process typically takes 30-45 minutes.

## Benefits

- Stops decay from progressing further into the tooth
- Restores tooth strength and normal chewing function
- Tooth-colored materials blend seamlessly with your natural teeth
- Prevents the need for more extensive treatment later
