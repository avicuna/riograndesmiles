## What to Expect

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. The process begins with placing a titanium post into the jawbone, which serves as a sturdy foundation. After a healing period of a few months, Dr. Vicuna will attach a custom-made crown that looks and functions like a natural tooth.

## Benefits

- Permanent solution that can last a lifetime with proper care
- Looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth
- Preserves jawbone health and prevents bone loss
- No impact on adjacent healthy teeth, unlike bridges
