## What to Expect

A dental crown restores a damaged tooth to its full strength and appearance. During your first visit, Dr. Vicuna will prepare the tooth and take impressions for a custom crown. A temporary crown is placed while your permanent one is crafted. At the follow-up visit, the permanent crown is cemented in place for a perfect, long-lasting fit.

## Benefits

- Restores strength to cracked, weakened, or heavily filled teeth
- Custom-made to match the color and shape of your natural teeth
- Protects teeth after root canal treatment
- Durable and long-lasting, typically 10-15 years or more
