## What to Expect

Veneers are a popular cosmetic option for transforming your smile. Dr. Vicuna will prepare your teeth by removing a thin layer of enamel, take precise impressions, and place temporary veneers while your custom set is crafted. Once ready, the permanent veneers are bonded to your teeth for a beautiful, lasting result.

## Benefits

- Dramatically improves the appearance of chipped, stained, or uneven teeth
- Porcelain veneers resist staining better than natural teeth
- Minimally invasive procedure with stunning results
- Long-lasting with proper care, often 10-15 years or more
