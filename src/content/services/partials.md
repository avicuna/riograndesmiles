## What to Expect

Partial dentures are designed to work with your remaining natural teeth. Dr. Vicuna will take impressions and design a partial that clasps securely to your existing teeth while filling in the gaps. The process involves a few visits for fitting and adjustments to ensure a perfect, comfortable fit.

## Benefits

- Fills gaps from missing teeth for a complete smile
- Prevents remaining teeth from shifting out of position
- Removable for easy cleaning and maintenance
- More affordable alternative to implants or bridges
