## What to Expect

Getting dentures is a collaborative process. Dr. Vicuna will take precise impressions of your mouth and work with a dental lab to create dentures that fit comfortably and look natural. You'll have follow-up appointments to fine-tune the fit, and we'll teach you how to care for your new dentures.

## Benefits

- Restores your ability to eat and speak with confidence
- Custom-fitted for comfort and a natural appearance
- Supports facial structure and prevents sagging
- Available as full or partial options to suit your needs
