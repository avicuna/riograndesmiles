## What to Expect

A dental bridge typically requires two visits. During the first appointment, Dr. Vicuna will prepare the adjacent teeth and take impressions for your custom bridge. A temporary bridge protects your teeth while the permanent one is crafted. At the second visit, your permanent bridge is placed and adjusted for a comfortable, secure fit.

## Benefits

- Permanently replaces one or more missing teeth
- Restores your ability to chew and speak normally
- Maintains the natural shape of your face
- Prevents remaining teeth from drifting out of position
