## What to Expect

Your comfort is our priority during any extraction. Dr. Vicuna will ensure the area is completely numb before beginning. Most simple extractions take 20-30 minutes, and you'll receive detailed aftercare instructions to ensure smooth healing.

## Benefits

- Relieves pain from severely decayed or damaged teeth
- Prevents infection from spreading to surrounding teeth
- Creates space for orthodontic treatment when needed
- Quick procedure with straightforward recovery
