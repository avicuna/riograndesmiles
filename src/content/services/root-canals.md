## What to Expect

Modern root canals are much more comfortable than you might expect. Dr. Vicuna will thoroughly numb the area, then carefully remove the infected tissue from inside the tooth. The tooth is cleaned, sealed, and typically fitted with a crown for lasting protection. Most patients report feeling immediate relief from the pain that brought them in.

## Benefits

- Saves your natural tooth from extraction
- Eliminates pain caused by infection or deep decay
- Modern techniques make the procedure comfortable and efficient
- Protects surrounding teeth from the spread of infection
