## What to Expect

If your dentures are cracked, chipped, or have a broken clasp, we can often repair them quickly. Bring your damaged dentures to our office, and Dr. Vicuna will assess the damage and determine the best repair approach. Many repairs can be completed the same day so you won't be without your dentures for long.

## Benefits

- Fast turnaround, often same-day repairs
- Restores proper fit and function of your dentures
- More cost-effective than full replacement
- Extends the lifespan of your existing dentures
